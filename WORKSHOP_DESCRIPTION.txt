[b]Version 1.1.0-dev.7[/b]

Build tall wooden, military, security, razor-wire and wrought-iron fences with vanilla Project Zomboid art. Choose sections, corners and standalone posts to suit your build, with a separate wrought-iron gate.

[b]Development build:[/b] This version is available for testing. Single-player and dedicated multiplayer validation are still pending. Use a disposable test world and back up existing saves.

[h2]Fence styles and skills[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Tall Wooden Straight Top[/b] - Carpentry 4
[*][b]Tall Wooden Sloped Top[/b] - Carpentry 7
[*][b]Security and Razor-Wire Security[/b] - Welding 4
[*][b]Military and Wrought Iron[/b] - Welding 7
[/list]
Security and razor-wire sections use mixed concrete: one use for straight or post-ended sections, two for corners. A full metal or carved mixed-concrete bucket contains four uses.

[h2]Choose your fence parts[/h2]
Each fence family has one build-menu entry. Press [b]E[/b] or the right controller bumper to cycle forward, and [b]Q[/b] or the left bumper to cycle back through the family's available parts and orientations. Every part retains its own materials, tools and skill requirement. The selected part's requirements update as you cycle.

The separate Wrought Iron Gate offers west and north placement. Use normal gate controls to open it after construction.

[h2]Sandbox setting[/h2]
[b]Construction Health Percent[/b] controls the health of newly built fence sections and corners. The default is 100%; multiplayer uses the server setting. Changing it leaves existing fences unchanged.

[h2]Known issues[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tall wooden fences may cut away prematurely.
[*]Standalone posts allow passage, but may overlap the player visually on the same tile.
[*]Collision, sounds, material consumption, save/reload and multiplayer synchronization still need in-game testing.
[*]Legacy fence definitions are retained; compatibility with existing saves is untested.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a problem[/h2]
Restart the server and clients after updating, and confirm that everyone loads the same version. Include your game build, mod list, steps to reproduce and relevant client/server logs when reporting a problem.
