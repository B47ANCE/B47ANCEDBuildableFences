[b]Version 1.1.0-dev.12[/b]

Build tall wooden, military, security, razor-wire and wrought-iron fences, plus missing ornamental fences, pickets, sheet-metal panels, balustrades, barriers, railings and gates using vanilla Project Zomboid art. Choose sections, corners and standalone posts to suit your build, with a separate wrought-iron gate.

[b]Development build:[/b] This version is available for testing. Single-player and dedicated multiplayer validation are still pending. Use a disposable test world and back up existing saves.

Security and razor-wire sections use mixed concrete: one use for straight or post-ended sections, two for corners. A full metal or carved mixed-concrete bucket contains four uses.

[h2]Choose your fence parts[/h2]
Each fence or railing family has one build-menu entry. New entries cover only artwork without vanilla recipes; existing vanilla recipes remain available. Press [b]E[/b] or the right controller bumper to cycle forward, and [b]Q[/b] or the left bumper to cycle back through the family's available parts and orientations. Every part retains its own materials, tools and skill requirement. The selected part's requirements update as you cycle.

The separate Wrought Iron Gate offers west and north placement. Added gate styles include shared tall wood (both fence tops), white picket, ornamental iron and green field gates, with wide variants where vanilla assets exist. Use normal gate controls after construction. Existing vanilla wire, pole and wooden gate recipes are retained.

[h2]Sandbox settings[/h2]
Separate [b]automatic junction post[/b] settings for wrought iron, military, sloped-top wood and straight-top wood add vanilla-style posts when matching perpendicular sections of the same family meet. Security and razor-wire keep their existing manual corners. Included in the unchanged section cost. Enabled by default and controlled by the server. Disabling it stops new automatic posts; existing objects remain.

[b]Construction Health Percent[/b] controls the health of newly built fence sections and corners. The default is 100%; multiplayer uses the server setting. Changing it leaves existing fences unchanged.

[h2]New fence and railing styles[/h2]
Ornamental iron, white and round-top pickets, low wood, post-and-wire, additional chain-link parts, sheet-metal panels, stone balustrades, razor coils, crowd barriers and military gabion barriers. Railing groups include wooden and metal balusters, handrails, panel rails and decorative stair components. Parts retain their own recipes and skill requirements, shown during selection.

[h2]Known issues[/h2]
Stair-railing fragments and loose-wire components are decorative where vanilla supplies no complete barrier geometry. They do not add automatic stair assembly or new collision. Four tile definitions without installed artwork are excluded. Wide-gate operation and railing placement require in-game testing.

Automatic junctions are created only during new construction. Existing fences are not retrofitted, and removing a section does not automatically remove its junction post. Native collision/save/multiplayer validation remains pending.
